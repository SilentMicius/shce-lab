# SHCE Lab

**Spatial/Homing Communication Evolution Laboratory**

SHCE Lab is a research laboratory for generating, simulating, stress-testing and ranking candidate architectures for geospatial communication, localization and autonomous homing.

## Research principle

The laboratory separates:

1. **Hypothesis generation** — candidate architectures are represented as an Architecture Genome.
2. **Physics validation** — impossible or unsupported assumptions are rejected before ranking.
3. **Simulation** — synthetic environments are used for reproducible experiments.
4. **Red-team testing** — noise, sensor failure, map error and hostile conditions are introduced deliberately.
5. **Evolution** — promising architectures are mutated and recombined.
6. **Evidence registry** — every experiment and result receives a persistent identifier.

The first milestone is intentionally synthetic. A successful simulation is **not** evidence that a physical communication channel works in the real world; physical feasibility must later be established through measurement and controlled experiments.

## Initial research direction

The project explores combinations of environmental fingerprints, sensor fusion, spatial localization, adaptive routing and candidate physical carriers. The long-term goal is to determine whether a robust communication/localization architecture can exploit passive and active geospatial information without assuming conventional global positioning as its sole reference.
